# Curiosity Club yellow theme

Created 2026-10-22 as a separate copy of the current Explained v0.1.5 source.

Palette: cream `#FFFDF4`, card surface `#FFFEF9`, charcoal `#20201D`, secondary text `#626157`, yellow `#F0E56B`, pale yellow `#FFF9CE`.

This copy retains the Yellow palette and reading layouts. In v0.2.0, its transport uses official DeepSeek APIs from the extension background with a user-provided key. No WASM or offscreen permission remains. Consent and reading controls retain their Yellow behavior. See README.md for installation and validation boundaries.

## Icon

The owner selected direction D (highlight) on 2026-10-22: a cream rounded tile, charcoal outline and italic `e.`, with a yellow highlight behind the letter. `icons/mark.svg` preserves the approved vector; transparent PNG exports at 16, 32, 48 and 128 pixels serve the manifest, toolbar, popup and welcome screen.
