A LITTLE MORE UNDERSTANDING

Meet your reading companion.

A clearer meaning. A little more context. Right beside what you’re reading.

A little context, with your permission.

With lookups enabled, clicking the selection icon or pressing Ctrl+X (⌘X on Mac) sends your selected text to DeepSeek for an explanation. Switching to News searches for recent developments. Explanations and searches use DeepSeek’s official APIs with your own key. DeepSeek handles submitted text under its API policies. “Explain a little more” also starts a web search. Pause lookups anytime from the toolbar. Privacy & permissions.

One key powers explanations and web search. Kept only in memory for this browser session. Re-enter after restarting Chrome or reloading/updating the extension. API calls are billed to your DeepSeek account. Get an API key ↗

Enabling verifies your key without sending reading text or making a paid lookup.

  3. 3Follow your curiosity.Switch to News for recent developments, or choose “Explain a little more” for a deeper explanation with web sources.
